How powerful is the bot?
20 one-year simulations, 11 paired configurations

Every simulation is an independent twelve-month market — 40 symbols, 5-minute bars, 105,120 candles each — traded by an engine that mirrors server/src/trader.ts rule-for-rule: same scanner gates, same sizing and slot logic, same TP/SL ladder, same fees, slippage, funding and liquidation. Four worlds are bull, four bear-tilted, four ranging, four volatile and four crisis. Every variant runs on the same price paths, so comparisons are paired.

The engine is strong. The shipped strategy is not. The EMA(11)/EMA(34) cross on 5-minute candles fires ≈ 9.2×/day/symbol — a property of the filter, identical at 40 %, 100 % and 200 % annualised volatility — so the bot acts on ~2165 signals a month and pays 156 % of starting equity a year in taker fees and slippage against a measured edge of -0.072 R per trade. Result: 0/20 worlds profitable, 20/20 ruined, mean drawdown 100 %. Inverting every signal gives the same expectancy (-0.070 R) — there is no usable directional edge at this timeframe and cost level. But it is fixable with settings + one execution change: on 15-minute candles with maker fees the same bot reaches median +104% a year, 4/6 worlds profitable and zero ruined worlds.

8Bug found and fixed

server/src/trader.ts (paper mode): sizing equity was paperBalance + Σ realised PnL, but the paper balance already books every fill, so realised P&L was counted twice — over-sizing after wins, under-sizing after losses, the opposite of what account.ts documents. Fixed on this branch (paper equity = balance + unrealised, the Binance definition). npm test and npm run test:e2e pass; the e2e paper balance is unchanged at 997.54 USDT.

9What to do, ranked by measured effect

  1. Trade the 15m candle (interval): 1013 → 286 trades/mo, fee bill 156 % → 170 % of equity.
  2. Get maker fills (post-only entries + limit TP) or a better fee tier: break-even for the 5m strategy is 0.017 %/side.
  3. Stop reversing on every opposite cross — it converts chop into fees (-0.018 R vs -0.072 R).
  4. Cap concurrency at 2–3 slots and 2–3 % margin — the 8-slot default can reach ~4× equity notional; slots2 cut mean DD from 100 % to 96 %.
  5. Keep the risk machinery — ladder, breakeven moves, ownership guards and the live-arming sequence all behaved correctly here and in the repo's suites.
  6. Re-run on real Binance klines before funding — node simulation/run.js --data ./klines. Synthetic results are promising, not proof.

10Method & limits

  • Synthetic markets (no exchange egress in this sandbox), generated by a documented regime-switching GARCH model and validated against published crypto stylised facts; the identical harness runs on real klines with --data.
  • Conservative fill model: inside a candle the stop is assumed hit before the take-profit — measured to matter in only 0.02 % of trades; stops are gap-honest.
  • Not modelled (all would worsen live results): order rejections, latency, exchange downtime, partial fills, rate limits, funding spikes.
  • 20 worlds give a distribution, not a guarantee — see the per-world table for the dispersion.
  • Not financial advice. The software places real orders when armed; the authors accept no liability.
